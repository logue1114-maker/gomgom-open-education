# Grade3 double and half through100

Selected Namibia NIED Junior Primary printed page9 requirements: double inputs1–100 (results through200), halve even inputs2–100 and odd inputs1–99 with remainder. Existing objectDouble/objectHalf/objectHalfRemainder are reused. A typed doubleHalfDecomposition rule selects a nontransferring blank strategy: learner-entered tens and ones parts, doubled or halved parts, sum and optional remainder. All values must be entered by the learner. This covers100/50/50 finite numeric facts, deliberately repeats after exhaustion, and does not assert physical or oral performance or worldwide completion. Earlier grade bounds and guides are retained.

Tests: `gradle :math:engine:test --tests com.gomgomapps.math.core.DoubleHalfGrade3Test --tests com.gomgomapps.math.core.DoubleHalfTest --tests com.gomgomapps.math.core.NamibiaPrimaryTest`. All200 inputs are independently solved from public givens; tests cover both carry and noncarry, odd1/remainder,100 endpoints, no early repeat, answer-key-independent help and lower-grade bounds.

Official source: https://www.nied.edu.na/assets/documents/02Syllabuses/02JuniorPrimary/01Syllabuses/02English/JP_Mathematicssyllabus(English)2024.pdf . Android UI, ads, billing, actual student records, QA and APKs are excluded.
