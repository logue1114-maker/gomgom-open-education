# Subtraction decomposition through20

Namibia Junior Primary Mathematics printed15 Grade1 includes breaking down and building up numbers for subtraction within0..20. The existing basic optional helper covered only first10..18, single-digit subtrahends crossing ten. New eligible subtraction problems now also split the first number into10 and its remaining part for other single-digit subtrahends, or split a two-digit subtrahend into tens and ones and subtract those parts in order. This adds109 public pairs (54 single-digit,55 two-digit) alongside45 existing ten-crossing pairs; zero/equal-amount facts remain separate. This selected numeric strategy does not establish all representations, fluency, oral/physical execution or whole competency.

Question identity, answer, normal topic and main submission remain unchanged. Every helper field starts empty and learners enter all parts and intermediate values. The final helper answer is not transferred. New extended guides use whole-subtract-parts-v2. Saved original problems with whole-number-relations-v1 rebuild their original three-step guide from public operands, preserving stage and learner entries without accepting cached expected-answer metadata. Existing five-step crossing guides also retain their meanings. Newly generated eligible problems use the extended guide; serialized new guides retain their current unfinished entry.
