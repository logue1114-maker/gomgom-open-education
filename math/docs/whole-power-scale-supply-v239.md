# Whole-number power-of-ten supply

The generator shuffles eligible scales before selecting an unseen question within a scale. Exhausted small scales defer to scales with unseen questions; after all eligible questions are exhausted, the oldest eligible question may repeat. Public operand coverage, exact decimal division, curriculum limits and learner storage contracts remain unchanged.

Verification: 24 seeds for each of two skills, 100 normal generated questions per seed, all six scales reached without repeated questions; exhausted million-scale and narrow-limit regressions. These checks establish supply behavior, not student fluency or country curriculum completion.
