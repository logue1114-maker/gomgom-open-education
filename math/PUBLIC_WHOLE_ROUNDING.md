Public whole rounding checks
============================

Whole rounding, ceiling, truncation and rounded addition reconstruct results from displayed givens and target units. Saved answers and hidden expressions do not determine checking. Existing supply, help/version, blank calculation fields and no answer transfer remain. Numeric equivalents remain accepted; malformed inputs request correction.

Tests cover 28000 selected cases across seven units, independent integer rounding oracles, upper boundaries through ten million, invalid inputs,150 fresh Grade6 questions and existing decimal rounding regressions. This is not exhaustive coverage of all integers, languages, physical devices or worldwide curricula. Native QA and APKs remain private.
