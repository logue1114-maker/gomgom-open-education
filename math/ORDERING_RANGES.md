# Selected Namibia ordering ranges

NIED Junior Primary printed page8: Grade2 order numbers0–100 and identify ordinal positions1–20; Grade3 order numbers0–500 and identify positions1–30. Reuses numberOrder and objectOrdinal with four new grade placements. This selected exercise orders three distinct numbers in ascending or descending order and counts a marked object's position from either side. Ordinal names, full ordinal sequences, physical activities and longer sorting tasks are not complete.

The generator decodes indexed public conditions instead of allocating the cubic ordering domain on every phone question. Grade2/3 sorting has1999800/249999000 public ordered presentations, not that many arithmetic facts. Ordinal pictures have420/930 conditions. Existing first-grade ordering/ordinal bounds and identity remain unchanged. Public-geometry-based help never transfers the answer.
