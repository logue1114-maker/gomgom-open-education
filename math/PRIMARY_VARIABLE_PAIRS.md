# Selected elementary two-variable pairs

Find any nonnegative integer pair satisfying ax+by=c, coefficients1..6 and right side2..36. Only public equations with at least two valid pairs are supplied. A dedicated checker validates the learner pair against the public equation; no preferred answer pair is stored or compared. Incorrect pairs cannot identify a particular mistaken variable. Noninteger/negative/empty input is distinguished from mathematical error.

Three optional blank frames identify both coefficients and the right side; they do not choose x or y or transfer a result. Full pair-search scaffolding remains to be added. The finite supply selects unused displayed equations before repetition. Tests independently enumerate all valid pairs for every supplied equation, check incorrect and invalid inputs, poison stored keys, and check100fresh conditions.

Selected England Year6 mappings reuse one-letter expression recognition/substitution/equations and whole-number linear sequence types. They are partial coverage of formulae, algebraic expression and generation/description requirements. Complete enumeration of all two-variable combinations remains missing; senior simultaneous equations are not a substitute.

Private Android UI/translations/records/QA/APKs/ads/billing are excluded. These source tests do not prove physical-device behavior, learning effectiveness, complete national coverage or a Play release.
