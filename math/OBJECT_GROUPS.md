# Object grouping and equal sharing foundations

Three public families: objectGroupTotal (equal-group total), objectShare (exact equal share), objectGroupRemainder (full groups and leftover objects). Prompts, blank help and diagrams do not require multiplication or division notation. Empty sharing spaces show only the public recipients, never a prefilled quotient. Help is reconstructed from public prompt givens and cannot transfer an answer to the main problem.

NIED Junior Primary 2024 printed pp.16–17 has selected Grade1/2 mappings. Grade1: totals through20 with group sizes2/5/10; exact sharing through20 among2/3/4/5; full groups/remainders through10 with sizes2/3/4/5. Grade2: quantities through50 with sizes2/3/4/5/10. Group totals are limited to10 groups for readable cards.

Finite distinct public conditions per Grade1/Grade2: total16/45, exact share25/68, grouping with leftovers30/231. A100-question study may necessarily revisit a small finite domain; the generator exhausts unused published conditions before revisiting. No artificial IDs, unsupported larger quantities or fixed answer slots are used to claim100 unique problems. Wider numeric outcomes, oral/physical strategies, remainder fractions, contextual remainder decisions and the rest of the curriculum remain incomplete.
