Prime vocabulary practice
=========================

England Year5 selected practice classifies integers 1..144 as prime, composite or neither, and tests whether a candidate is both prime and a divisor. The prime-factor pool includes actual divisors and small prime distractors. 1 is neither prime nor composite. Composite factors are not prime factors. Checking derives values from the public prompt, ignores stored keys, and never fills learner answers from help. Help checks learner-entered positive-factor counts and remainders before a vocabulary choice. Indexed supply avoids repeated conditions until exhaustion; choices shuffle in ordinary Generator flow.

Four engine tests independently solve every bounded condition, poison hidden keys, cover 1/2/121 and composite/non-divisor candidates, malformed prompts, blank help/no transfer, fresh supply/oldest reuse, choice shuffling and Year5-only placement. This does not claim worldwide curriculum completion, all-language or physical-device verification, or Play readiness. Android UI, translations, records, QA and APK remain private.
