Year6 contextual calculation connections
=======================================

Four existing basic families are now also available in England Grade6: factor-based exact bags, minimum batch production, repeated square tile boards and cube packing. Existing Grade5 placements, numeric domains, public-given checkers, blank non-transfer help and translations remain. Together with existing two-change addition/subtraction and contextual division this improves selected four-operation problem coverage; it does not complete every contextual requirement.

Five core tests cover every existing family condition,150 fresh problems per family at Grade6, independently recomputed answers, poisoned stored keys, incorrect answers and blank help. One tile-board family was exercised through normal Android Grade6 Topic practice, wrong answer/help, cold restoration, no transfer, direct correction and history452to453; other families remain native unverified in this bundle. All devices/languages, larger numeric domains, wider contexts, learning outcomes, worldwide curricula and Play release remain.
