# Convenient-number mental arithmetic

Selected EN-Y5-08: two public operands 1,000..999,999 and a public rounding unit from 10 to 1,000,000. The second operand is 1..9 away from its nearest multiple; ties choose the larger multiple. Addition adjusts the intermediate sum. Subtraction first adjusts the first operand by the same amount, avoiding negative intermediate values. Five blank steps ask for the first number, convenient number, gap, intermediate value and final result. No answer transfer. Existing strategies remain separate.

Eight engine tests cover 300 fresh problems per direction, independent correction and poisoned-key checks, all six units, ties, zero differences, small domains exhausted before oldest reuse, sampled large indexes and prior strategies. Full large-domain exhaustion, explanations, fluency and worldwide curriculum coverage are not claimed. UI, translations, records, native QA and APKs remain private.
