# Fractional lengths in stories

Selected England Year3 EN-Y3-20 practice adds cord length contexts with like-denominator fractions within one whole. Total cord used has286 distinct conditions; cord remaining has363, including zero remaining. Denominators2..12 are a selected app range. Each unit exhausts fresh conditions before oldest reuse.

Students enter the denominator and both given numerators, then compute the result numerator in four initially blank help steps. No final answer is transferred. Students fill numerator and denominator themselves in the main answer. Checking and error-part localization derive from the visible story, ignore stored keys and accept exact equivalent fractions. Incorrect or malformed, unequal-denominator, beyond-one-whole and negative-remainder public conditions are rejected.

Three engine tests cover all649 conditions, equivalent answers, wrong numerator localization, blank help, poisoned keys, real finite exhaustion and invalid stories. App translations, UI, student records, native QA, APKs and monetization remain private. Other contexts, fraction comparison/equivalence applications, broader representations, worldwide coverage, fluency and Play release readiness remain incomplete.
