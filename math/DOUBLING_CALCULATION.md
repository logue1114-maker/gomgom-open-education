# Mental multiplication and division by doubling/halving

Selected England EN-Y3-12 mental-method practice adds two units alongside the existing distributive products, carry-based written products and known-table statements. Multiplication uses a two-digit multiplicand10..99 and factor2,4 or8. Division uses the corresponding multiple and divisor2,4 or8, giving a two-digit whole quotient10..99. Each unit has270 distinct public conditions and exhausts fresh conditions before oldest reuse. Existing unit/country ranges are unchanged; explicit new mapping is Year3.

Students enter the starting number, multiplier/divisor and every intermediate double or half. There are one to three successive operations. The scaffold shows previous+previous or previous÷2, with only a checked previous student entry available as a reference. No question-specific calculation is prefilled and the final result is never transferred. Checking derives the answer from strict valid public operands and ignores hidden keys.

Tests independently cover all540 conditions, each intermediate expectation/no transfer, actual270-per-unit fresh exhaustion/oldest reuse and Year3 mapping, plus malformed operands. Existing whole-integer unit generation/help remains regression evidence. UI, locales, student drafts, native QA and APKs remain private.
