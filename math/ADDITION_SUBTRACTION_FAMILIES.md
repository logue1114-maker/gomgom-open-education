Addition and subtraction families
=================================

The same whole and two parts connect one addition and two subtractions.
A given addition leads to two related subtractions; a given subtraction leads
to the inverse addition and the other subtraction. Learners enter both results.

NIED Junior Primary printed p.15 section2.2 explicitly relates subtraction
to addition in Grades1–2. Selected Grade1 whole/parts stay0..20; Grade2 stays0..99.
Five blank non-transfer teaching frames identify whole/parts and calculate
the two inverse relationships. Checkers derive results from public equations
and reject inconsistent facts; stored answer keys are not used for marking.

The indexed domain includes10100 Grade2 conditions, including zero parts,
equal parts, both directions and boundaries. Tests independently calculate
both public equations, check wrong-field identification, poisoned keys,
blank help, grade limits and150 fresh draws for each grade.

Concrete models, other strategies, oral fluency, all competency strands,
all languages/devices, learning efficacy and Play release remain separate.
This module does not imply whole-curriculum completion.
