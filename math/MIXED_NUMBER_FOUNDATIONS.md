Mixed-number foundations
========================

Existing mixed-to-improper and improper-to-mixed IDs and numeric answer slots are retained. Conversion answers are now derived from visible givens rather than saved keys. A new England Year5 topic asks students to complete a like-denominator proper-fraction sum greater than one as both an improper fraction and a mixed number. Three numeric slots enter the improper numerator, whole part and remaining numerator, with the denominator fixed in the public statement. No solved values are prefilled.

Selected denominator range2..12 supplies396 conditions per conversion and220 sums,1012 total. Default conversion range2..9 retains216 conditions each; other explicit packs retain their selected denominators within2..12. Tests exhaust the selected conditions, poison saved keys, independently calculate answers and exercise each wrong slot; each type supplies100 fresh questions. Optional help has5/4/6 initially empty relationship frames and cannot transfer answers. Existing conversion teaching-version IDs are retained for student draft compatibility. Numeric-equivalent answers are accepted; missing or invalid values ask for input. Only the new sum topic is restricted to its reviewed pack placement.

These selected computational foundations do not establish all representations, recognition, naming, fluency or full national/global curriculum coverage. Android UI, translations, native QA, learner records and APKs remain private.
