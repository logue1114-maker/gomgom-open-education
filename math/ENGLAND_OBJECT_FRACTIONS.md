# Selected England Year1 objects and Year2 written shape fractions

DfE Year1: one-step multiplication/division with objects/pictures; recognise/find/name halves and quarters of objects/shapes/quantities. Year2: recognise/find/name/write1/3,1/4,2/4,3/4 and simple fractions, including2/4=1/2. Source: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study

Four selected placements reuse existing suppliers/help/UI. Year1 grouping/share totals up to20 using2/5/10 (selected app drill bounds, not an official numeric maximum), and choosing a half/quarter picture. Year2 writes the colored fraction from equal-area cells, using the displayed parts1/2,1/3,1/4,2/4,3/4. Optional partFractions keeps unreduced presented pairs distinct, checks actual visible cells, and leaves other curricula unchanged. Student-filled help never transfers an answer. Equivalent fractional answers remain accepted, while decimal answers do not replace writing a fraction in the selected Year2 task.

Finite domains:16 each grouping/sharing;64 half/quarter picture conditions including orientation/distractor layouts;17 Year2 colored layouts across5 presented part pairs. These are finite conditions, not100 unique arithmetic facts. Exhaustion repeats the oldest condition. Picture answer positions are shuffled. Whole quantities, lengths, objects, fraction names/equivalence teaching, oral/real-object/teacher support and full curriculum remain partial. No official example problem wording is copied. Private UI/translations/ads/billing/APKs/learner records/QA are excluded.
