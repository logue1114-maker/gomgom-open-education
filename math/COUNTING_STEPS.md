# Counting forwards, backwards and in steps

Public direction, step and starting number determine one empty next-number input. Blank non-transfer help asks the learner for start, step and next number; hidden answer metadata cannot supply the help. Positive direction stays inside the range; backwards includes zero. No negative number, operator notation or algebra prerequisite.

NIED Junior printed p.6 selected written next-number mappings:

Grade1: forward1 through100; backward1 from20; forward2 through30,5/10 through100. Grade2: forward1 through500; backward1 through30 and2 through20; forward2 through100,3 through30,4 through40,5/10 through500. Grade3: any valid starting number forward/backward,1 through1000,2 through500,3 through60,4 through80,5/10 through1000,20 through500,30 through180,50/100 through1000.

Finite conditions by Grade1/2/3: forward100/500/1000; backward20/40/6105; skip45/220/5105. Small facts repeat after exhaustion; no artificial IDs or larger ranges establish100unique conditions. Candidate numbers are enumerated without constructing every help plan; only the selected question receives its guide.

Written practice is not proof of orally reciting complete sequences, concrete-object counts through50/100, object rearrangement, classroom learning efficacy or complete curricula. Those remain incomplete. The reference syllabus examples are not copied.
