Inverse product facts
=====================

Selected NIED Junior Primary printed page17 Grade3 relation between division
and multiplication facts of2/3/5/10. Public dividend10..100, table quotient1..10.
64 ordered conditions connect a known product to a related quotient, or a known
quotient to a related product. Each public known equation must be arithmetically
valid; unrelated target equations are rejected. Answers and blank teaching frames
come from public givens, never stored answer keys. Learners identify the total,
quantity in a group and group count before entering the related calculation.
Helpers reference accepted inputs and do not transfer an answer to the main field.

Independent domain/poisoned-key/wrong-input/placement and full fresh-supply tests
cover64 conditions. Existing tables/divide contracts remain. This adds a reviewed
Grade3 pathway, not all division contexts/fluency or Grade2 no-division-symbol work.
Native Android evidence and app source remain private.
