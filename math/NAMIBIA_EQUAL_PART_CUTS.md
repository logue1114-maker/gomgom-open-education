Namibia Grade 1: selected straight rectangle cuts

Learners move a horizontal or vertical cut to make two equal areas. The checker derives areas from public dimensions and user position, rather than a stored answer. Thirty-six shape/orientation conditions support fresh supply. Only the selected Namibia Grade 1 mapping is enabled.

Independent tests compare every position 1 through 99 against area equality, reject missing/invalid input and poisoned stored answers, and check grade/fresh-supply boundaries. Android normal-menu checks verified both orientations, incorrect positions, direct correction, and saved progress. The private app, screenshots and learner data are excluded from this core repository.

This does not establish actual-object cutting, every shape, oral instruction, physical-device accessibility, or completion of the whole curriculum.
