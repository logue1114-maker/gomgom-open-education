# Year2 tens and ones

Selected EN-Y2-02: reuse existing place10 and wholePlaceValue for two-digit numbers10..99 and ones/tens. Learners distinguish a digit from the value it represents. Grade2 mappings and limits are added; upper-grade implementations are unchanged. Existing three/four student blank teaching steps derive quantities from the public number and named place, with no final answer transfer. The engine test checks100fresh conditions per type, both places, independent arithmetic, distractor positions, blank help and neighbouring grade boundaries.

This selected written numeric practice is partial coverage. Concrete/pictorial representations and all official outcomes are not claimed. Private UI, translations, student history, evidence, APKs, ads and billing are excluded. Tests do not prove physical devices, learning effectiveness or Play release.
