Year5 powers of ten
===================

Reuse decimalTimesPowersTen and decimalDividePowersTen for England Year5. Each supplies300000 exact conditions: inputs0..99.999 and factors/divisors10,100,1000. Division includes decimal inputs for every divisor and answers through six decimal places. Year6 keeps its previous selected three-place-answer domain (11100 division conditions). Wider bounds, contexts and learner fluency remain unproven.

Public numeric checking derives the result from the displayed operands, ignoring stored answer keys. Blank arithmetic help does not transfer its result to the main answer. Tests exhaust both selected Year5 domains, preserve Year6 bounds, exercise precision validation, poisoned keys, six-place answers, helper relationships and fresh normal generation. Decimal precision checks use exact rational arithmetic compatible with API26 rather than BigInteger.longValueExact. Android UI, translations, student data, native QA and APKs remain private.
