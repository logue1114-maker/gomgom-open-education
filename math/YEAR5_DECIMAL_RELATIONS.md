# Selected England Year5 decimal relations

Four explicit links reuse existing digit/value, decimal rounding and comparison skills and extend the three-card ordering component to decimals. Digit/value practice presents0.000..9.999 and ones/tenths/hundredths/thousandths:40,000 finite conditions. Decimal rounding presents0.00..99.99 to a whole number or one decimal place:20,000 finite conditions. Public-given help remains student-entered and does not transfer answers.

Finite rounding and digit/value supply now indexes conditions and constructs only selected questions instead of rebuilding every question/guide on each draw. Random fresh draws are followed by a complete finite walk if needed; reuse picks the least-recent condition only after exhaustion. The old Year4 numeric/presentation bounds are retained.

Comparison permits displayed decimal values through three places and includes equality and nearby same-whole values. Three decimal cards are chosen into ascending/descending positions with three student choices in help. Large comparison/ordering domains retain sampled freshness rather than claiming an exhaustive pool. New decimal ordering is excluded from unreviewed/default curriculum fallback.

Selected EN-Y5-28/29/30 components remain partial: verbal reading/writing, fraction connections and full contextual/representation outcomes are not completed by these symbolic drills. Tests independently check40,000 digit/value conditions,20,000 rounding conditions, boundary help, comparison and ordering from visible values, indexed supply/exhaustion and Year4/fraction regressions. Private UI, localization, learner records and release artifacts are excluded.
