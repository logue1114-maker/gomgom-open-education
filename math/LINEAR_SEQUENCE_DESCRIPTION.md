# Composed verbal linear-sequence rule

England Year6 selected EN-Y6-30. From four visible signed integer terms the learner chooses add/subtract and supplies a positive step size. These two student inputs compose a verbal rule; this is not free-prose language grading. No direction or magnitude is preselected or provided. The checker derives both from the public sequence, ignoring hidden answer placeholders and distinguishing wrong operation, wrong interval and missing input.

Terms-100..100 and nonzero differences-12..12 give4356 distinct public conditions, including increasing/decreasing, negative terms and zero crossings.100fresh conditions are tested. Four optional student blanks find both terms, their signed difference and its absolute size. Relationship labels remain numeric-free; no final answer transfer. Existing saved-session answers store student direction and interval. Tests cover every condition, wrong components, invalid/missing input, poisoned answer keys, blank teaching and signed teaching draft restore.

Private UI, translations, records, evidence, APKs, ads and billing are excluded. Wider linear contexts, free written explanations, national curriculum completion, physical devices, learning effectiveness and Play release remain unproven.
