# Counting backwards through zero

Selected England Year4 EN-Y4-03 practice asks students to write three consecutive missing numbers. Steps1..10 and starts-20..twice-the-step give320 distinct conditions.120 start at or above zero and cross into negatives by the third entry;200 start in the negative range and continue backwards. Existing nonnegative counting remains unchanged.

Checking derives all three entries from the public start and step, ignoring stored keys/expressions. Only the first incorrect entry is identified. Five initially blank help steps ask for the start, step and three successive results using generic role relationships. No final answer is transferred. Students enter the minus sign themselves.

Three tests cover all320 conditions and wrong-entry positions, blank help/no transfer, actual finite supply exhaustion before oldest reuse, and invalid public prompts.
