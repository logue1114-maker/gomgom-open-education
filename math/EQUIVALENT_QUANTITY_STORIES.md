Fraction rates reuse the2345visible conditions from FractionPercentEquivalence. Decimal rates0.000..1.000 and percentage rates0.0..100.0 each have1001conditions. Decimal-to-percentage has1001conditions, with an out-of100 choice and three student fields; fractional percentages and0/100boundaries are included.
