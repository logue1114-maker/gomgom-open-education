Year5 mental multiplication and division
=======================================

Existing convenient factor regrouping, doubling and halving are now explicitly available in England Year5 topic practice. Previous Year3 doubling/halving and Year4 regrouping placements are retained. Each doubling/halving type supplies270 distinct public operand conditions; regrouping has over100 independent triples. Learners enter their own numbers; help begins blank and cannot transfer answers. No duplicate type or expanded operand domain is introduced.

The new curriculum-level tests exercise100 fresh questions for each type, independent public arithmetic with poisoned keys, blank help/no transfer and precise earlier-grade placement. This is selected practice; wider strategies and learner fluency remain unproven. Android/student/QA artifacts remain private.
