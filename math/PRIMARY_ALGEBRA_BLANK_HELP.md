# Elementary substitution: learner-entered numbers

The three teaching frames ask for the value of x, the other number, and the result. The final frame displays a relationship label instead of automatically inserting the two given numbers. Only entries supplied by the learner may be displayed as completed steps.

The existing teaching version, expected arithmetic, frame count and draft format remain unchanged. A regression check covers addition, subtraction, multiplication and exact division, existing two-step draft restoration, incorrect results and a poisoned answer key. The existing generated-expression check also runs.

This bounded change does not add curriculum mappings, sequence generation or two-variable pair enumeration. It does not establish whole-curriculum completion or a Play release. Android UI, translations, records, ads, billing, APKs and private QA are excluded from this source publication.
