# Selected England Year1 numerical fraction quantities

Year1 recognises/finds/names halves and quarters of objects, shapes and quantities (DfE statutory mathematics programme): https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study

This delta connects the existing numerical quantity supplier and student-filled relationship help. Whole amounts are positive multiples of2 or4 up to20, unit fractions1/2 or1/4. The bound20 is an app drill selection, not an official mandated maximum. There are15 finite displayed facts; exhaust before repeating the oldest. Five blank stages ask the student for total, numerator, denominator, one-part quantity and required quantity. No computed answer is transferred to the body. Current-year practice is excluded from initial learned-only diagnosis.

This does not complete object/length representations, fraction names, Year2 non-unit quantities, or explicit2/4=1/2 teaching. Existing shape/picture practice is separate. Private UI/ads/billing/APKs/learner records/QA are excluded. No official example is copied.
