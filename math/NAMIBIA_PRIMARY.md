# Namibia primary calculation foundations

The selected NIED 2024 pack covers Grade 1–7 number and calculation practice. It is a partial alignment, not the complete national curriculum. Junior references are [the official Grade 1–3 syllabus](https://www.nied.edu.na/assets/documents/02Syllabuses/02JuniorPrimary/01Syllabuses/02English/JP_Mathematicssyllabus(English)2024.pdf); senior references are [the official Grade 4–7 syllabus](https://www.nied.edu.na/assets/documents/02Syllabuses/03SeniorPrimary/Mathematics/SP_Mathematicssyllabus2024.pdf). The TSV records printed page references. Official questions and PDF contents are not redistributed.

Counting to 20 uses visible 5-by-4 dot cards with varied occupied positions. It retains the existing exhaustive 3-by-3 counting domain for bounds through 9. Number bonds, place value, whole-number arithmetic, fractions, decimals, factors and percentages reuse existing independently authored generators. Grade 1 excludes symbolic comparison, multiplication, division and fractions. Grade 2 introduces the specified multiplication tables and halves/quarters; division notation starts in Grade 3. Selected constraints set taught ranges and prevent negative answers. Grade 4 like-denominator fraction operations stay within one whole. Grade 7 decimal products have at most three decimal places, and decimal divisors have at most one.

`minGiven=0` allows general comparison below 100 while preserving its old default lower bound. `answerDecimalPlaces` and `secondDecimalPlaces` enforce precision through exact rational arithmetic. These options do not affect packs without the options. Counting layout signatures represent actual visible conditions rather than added question IDs.

Run `gradle :math:engine:test --tests '*NamibiaPrimaryTest'` with JDK 17+ and Gradle 8.14.3. The test exercises 100 generated questions per placement, diagnostic boundaries, count-card geometry and first-100 diversity, independently solved comparisons, and invalid fraction/decimal precision. One hundred generated questions is not a claim that every finite domain contains 100 unique facts. Finite tables or unit fractions can recur; presentation-only IDs are not counted as new questions.

Remaining work includes requirement-by-requirement numeric gap review, grouping without symbolic operations, word problems, geometry, measurement, statistics, other strands, senior-school curricula, regional/local-language review and device/long-session validation. Android UI, ads, billing, learner records, source PDFs, QA captures and APKs remain private.
